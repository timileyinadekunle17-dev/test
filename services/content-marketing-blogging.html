Frequently asked questions

Why does an ecommerce business need content marketing?

Useful content can help explain products, answer customer questions and support discovery across relevant search topics. Its value depends on the audience and the quality of the information.

Do you write product and blog content?

The service scope includes content planning, topic research, blog and landing-page structure, on-page guidance and editorial recommendations. Specific writing deliverables should be agreed for each project.

Can content marketing guarantee Google rankings?

No. Search visibility depends on competition, relevance, technical conditions and many other factors. Content should be created to help people first, with SEO supporting discoverability.
