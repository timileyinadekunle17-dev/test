Frequently asked questions

What does Google Merchant Center do?

Google Merchant Center helps businesses provide product information to Google for eligible product experiences and advertising destinations, subject to Google's policies and availability.

Can you fix product feed disapprovals?

We can review diagnostics and product attributes to identify issues and recommend or implement corrections within the agreed scope. Approval remains subject to Google's policies and review systems.

Is Merchant Center the same as Google Ads?

No. Merchant Center manages product data, while Google Ads manages advertising campaigns. They can work together for eligible shopping campaigns.
