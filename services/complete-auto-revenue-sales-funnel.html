Frequently asked questions

What is included in an ecommerce sales funnel?

Depending on the project, a funnel may include landing pages, lead capture, product information, checkout steps and relevant follow-up communication.

Can you automate follow-up messages?

We can review and configure agreed follow-up workflows using suitable tools available to your business. The channels and triggers depend on your platform and consent requirements.

Can a sales funnel guarantee more revenue?

No. Revenue depends on the offer, audience, traffic quality, pricing, customer experience and implementation. Funnel work focuses on organising and improving the customer journey.
