Klaviyo Email Marketing and Automation for Ecommerce

Email can help an ecommerce business communicate with subscribers and customers at important points in their relationship with the brand. De Impresa Globals organises Klaviyo foundations, signup forms, customer segments, automated flows and campaign templates around your customer journey and communication goals.

Build a clear subscriber-capture experience

We review how visitors can sign up and how signup points fit into the storefront. Forms and lists should support a clear reason to subscribe and make the next communication relevant to the customer.

Organise customer segments

Segmentation helps organise subscribers by relevant characteristics or interactions, where that information is available. We consider the structure needed to make campaign planning more organised and messages more relevant.

Set up useful customer lifecycle flows

Automated flows can support moments such as welcoming new subscribers, following up on abandoned carts, communicating after a purchase and re-engaging customers. The flow plan should reflect the store's customer journey and the data available.

Plan campaigns, test and hand over

We organise campaign templates and communication priorities, then review the setup before handover. The aim is a reusable foundation the business can maintain and refine.

Frequently asked questions

Is this service specifically for Klaviyo?

Yes. The service focuses on reviewing and organising Klaviyo for ecommerce email communication, including signup forms, segments, automated flows and campaign templates.

Which automated flows can be included?

The existing service scope identifies core flows such as welcome communication, abandoned-cart follow-up, post-purchase follow-up and customer re-engagement. The final selection depends on the store and project requirements.

Can you work with an existing Klaviyo account?

The service includes a Klaviyo integration review. Share details about your current setup and goals so the work can be scoped appropriately.

Will email marketing guarantee repeat purchases?

No specific sales outcome can be guaranteed. Results depend on the audience, offer, customer experience, list quality, consent and campaign execution. The service focuses on building an organised communication foundation.
