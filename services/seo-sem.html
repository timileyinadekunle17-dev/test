Start with search intent and page purpose

We review what potential customers are looking for and map relevant search themes to the pages that best answer those needs. This helps each service or product page focus on a clear topic instead of competing with other pages on the same site.

Strengthen technical and on-page foundations

The review can cover crawlability and indexing signals, site structure, page titles, descriptions, headings, internal links and page experience considerations. Recommendations are prioritised around the website and issues identified during the review.

Create useful pages for the right audience

Search visibility depends on pages that clearly explain the service or product. We help align page content with the questions, needs and language of potential customers, while keeping the content useful and natural rather than repeating keywords unnecessarily.

Coordinate organic and paid search activity

Where paid search is part of the brief, we consider campaign intent, landing-page relevance and measurement alongside organic priorities. SEO and paid search serve different roles, but both should support clear business objectives.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide SEO for Shopify stores?

The service is intended for ecommerce websites, including stores that need their search structure, page content and technical foundations reviewed. Share your platform and website URL so the scope can be assessed.

Is SEO the same as Google Ads?

No. SEO focuses on improving a website's visibility in organic search results. Google Ads is paid advertising. This service can consider both, but the work and measurement are different.

Can you guarantee first-page Google rankings?

No responsible SEO provider can guarantee a particular ranking. Search results depend on competition, website quality, search intent and factors outside a provider's control. The work focuses on sound optimisation and measurable progress.

How do you choose keywords for each page?

We consider the page's purpose, the services offered and the intent behind relevant searches. The aim is to assign each page a distinct primary topic and use related phrases naturally.
