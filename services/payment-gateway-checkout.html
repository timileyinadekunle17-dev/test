Frequently asked questions

Can you integrate payment gateways for a Nigerian ecommerce store?

We review providers supported by the store platform and the business's country, currency and eligibility. Provider availability and approval requirements must be confirmed for each business.

Can you set up international payments?

International payment support depends on the provider, business location, settlement options, currencies and compliance requirements. We assess the available options rather than assume every gateway is eligible.

Do you test checkout after setup?

The stated scope includes testing available purchase flows. Live payment tests must be planned carefully to avoid unintended charges and depend on provider test-mode support.
