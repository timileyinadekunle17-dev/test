Frequently asked questions

Which analytics platforms can be included?

The platforms and tags depend on your existing store, marketing stack and project requirements. We review the current setup before confirming which integrations are appropriate.

Can you check whether purchase tracking is working?

The service includes reviewing agreed conversion events and validating the available data. Access, platform configuration and consent settings can affect what can be tested.

Does analytics setup guarantee accurate attribution?

No tracking setup can remove every attribution limitation. We focus on correctly configured measurement within the capabilities and constraints of the platforms involved.
