Frequently asked questions

Why should my website use Google Search Console?

Search Console provides information about how Google crawls, indexes and displays a website in search. It can help identify technical issues and monitor organic search performance.

Can you submit my sitemap to Google?

Sitemap review and submission guidance are included in the service scope. A sitemap helps discovery but does not guarantee that every URL will be indexed.

Does Search Console improve rankings automatically?

No. It reports information and issues; improvements require appropriate changes to the website and content. Indexing and rankings are not guaranteed.
