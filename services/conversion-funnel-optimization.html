Frequently asked questions

What is ecommerce conversion rate optimisation?

Conversion rate optimisation is the process of understanding how visitors use a store and testing changes intended to make important actions easier to complete.

Which parts of a store do you review?

The service scope includes landing pages, product pages, cart and checkout, with priorities based on the store's customer journey and available information.

Can you guarantee a higher conversion rate?

No specific uplift can be guaranteed. Results depend on traffic, product-market fit, pricing, implementation and customer behaviour. Changes should be evaluated using reliable measurement.
