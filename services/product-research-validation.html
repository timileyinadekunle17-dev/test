Frequently asked questions

What does product research include?

The service scope includes niche and audience research, competitor review, demand assessment, opportunity comparison and a research summary.

Can you identify a guaranteed winning product?

No product can be guaranteed to succeed. Research can clarify demand signals and risks, but performance depends on execution, pricing, competition, supply and customer response.

Is product research suitable for an existing store?

Yes. Research can support decisions about adding products, reviewing a category or exploring a new audience, depending on the business question.
