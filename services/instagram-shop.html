Frequently asked questions

Can you connect my product catalogue to Instagram?

We can review your ecommerce platform and available Meta commerce connection, then confirm the supported setup and catalogue checks.

Can every business use Instagram Shopping?

Eligibility depends on Meta's commerce policies, supported markets, account status and product category. We check the applicable requirements before confirming the scope.

Do product tags guarantee sales?

No. Product tagging can make products easier to discover, but customer demand, product presentation, pricing and platform availability influence outcomes.
