Store structure built around how customers shop

We review the way your products and collections are organised, then plan navigation and page layouts that help visitors find relevant information. The goal is a store structure that is understandable and practical to maintain as your catalogue changes.

Product and collection pages that explain the offer

Product presentation should answer the questions a shopper needs resolved before making a decision. We organise product information, imagery areas, collection layouts and calls to action so the offer is easier to evaluate.

Consistent experience across desktop and mobile

We consider responsive layouts, readable content, clear navigation and usable controls across screen sizes. Your store's visual identity should remain consistent while the shopping experience adapts to the device.

A storefront ready for ongoing improvement

Design is part of the wider customer journey. We consider page structure, calls to action and useful product information so the store can be reviewed and improved as your business develops.

Frequently asked questions

Can you redesign an existing Shopify store?

Yes. The scope can focus on refreshing the visual design, improving navigation and page layouts, or reviewing the wider storefront experience. The exact work is agreed after reviewing your current store and requirements.

Do you work with platforms beyond Shopify?

The service page covers Shopify, Wix, Squarespace and WooCommerce storefronts. Tell us which platform your store uses when you request a project discussion.

Can you design for mobile shoppers?

Responsive design is part of the stated service scope. We consider how navigation, content, product information and controls work across desktop and mobile screen sizes.

What should I prepare before requesting a redesign?

Share your store URL, platform, the main issues you want addressed and any brand or design references. This helps establish a suitable scope for the project.
