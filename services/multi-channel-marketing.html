Frequently asked questions

Which marketing channels can be included?

The channel mix depends on your audience, products, budget and objectives. It may include search, social, email and content activity where those channels are suitable.

Should my business advertise on every platform?

No. A focused selection of relevant channels is often more practical than spreading resources too thinly. Channel choices should be based on audience fit and business priorities.

How do you measure multi-channel marketing?

We agree relevant actions and available tracking before activity begins, then review platform data and the customer journey with the limitations of attribution in mind.
