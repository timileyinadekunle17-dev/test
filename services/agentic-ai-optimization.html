Frequently asked questions

What is an agentic AI workflow for ecommerce?

It is a workflow in which an AI agent can perform selected steps using approved tools and instructions. The scope should define what it may do, what requires approval and when a person must take over.

Can an AI agent manage my entire online store?

We do not assume that full autonomy is appropriate. The service begins with specific use cases and sets permissions, boundaries and human review according to the task.

How do you decide whether a task is suitable for an AI agent?

We consider the task, systems involved, data access, failure risks, expected behaviour and the availability of human escalation before recommending a workflow.
