Ecommerce Google Ads Campaign Setup and Management

Google Ads can help put products and services in front of people actively searching or exploring relevant offers. De Impresa Globals plans ecommerce campaigns around your goals, audience, products and landing pages, with attention to measurement so campaign decisions can be reviewed using available data.

Review the account and campaign objectives

We begin by understanding the business goals, current advertising setup and products or services to promote. Where an account already exists, an initial review helps identify its structure and the areas that need attention.

Plan keywords, audiences and campaign structure

Campaign planning considers search themes, audience relevance, campaign types and the destinations people will reach after clicking an ad. The structure should reflect the offer and the action the business wants visitors to take.

Prepare ad messaging and landing-page alignment

Ad copy and destination pages should communicate a consistent offer. We plan messaging and review whether the relevant product or service pages provide the information visitors need to continue.

Review conversion measurement and optimisation opportunities

Tracking should help distinguish useful actions from general visits. We review available conversion measurement and use the data provided to identify practical optimisation opportunities. Advertising performance is not guaranteed and depends on budget, competition, offer and other factors.

Frequently asked questions

Do you manage Google Ads for ecommerce stores?

This service is designed around ecommerce objectives, products, audiences and relevant landing pages. The proposed scope is confirmed after understanding your store and advertising requirements.

Can you work with an existing Google Ads account?

Yes. The stated service includes an account review, which can help identify the current campaign structure and areas to investigate before changes are planned.

Can you guarantee sales or a return on ad spend?

No. Advertising outcomes depend on factors including budget, competition, product-market fit, pricing, website experience and tracking quality. We focus on campaign planning, measurement and informed optimisation.

Do I need conversion tracking?

Reliable measurement is important for evaluating campaign activity. The service includes a conversion tracking review so available measurement can be considered as part of the campaign plan.
