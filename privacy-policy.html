Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

De Impresa (“we”, “us” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, request an audit or quote, or engage us for ecommerce design, development, optimisation, SEO or digital marketing services.

1. Information we collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may collect:

  • Contact details: your name, email address, phone number, business name and the information you include in an enquiry.
  • Project information: website or store URLs, project requirements, brand materials, product information, access details you choose to provide, and communications about a project.
  • Payment and transaction information: payment status, transaction references and billing details made available to us. Payments may be handled by third-party payment providers; we do not intend to store full payment-card numbers or security codes.
  • Technical information: information such as IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited and approximate usage data, where collected through hosting logs, analytics or similar technologies.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, prepare proposals, deliver and support agreed services, communicate about projects, process or verify payments, maintain website security, improve our website and services, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

3. Lawful grounds and consent

Where applicable privacy law requires a lawful basis, we process information when it is necessary to perform a contract or take steps at your request, to pursue legitimate business interests, to comply with legal obligations, or when you have given consent. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it, subject to legal and contractual limitations.

4. Cookies and analytics

Our website or service providers may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, security, preferences and website analytics. You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website features. If we introduce non-essential cookies requiring consent in your location, we will request that consent where required.

5. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers who help us host and secure the website, communicate with clients, analyse website usage, process payments or deliver a project. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business reorganisation. Service providers are expected to use information only for the services they provide to us.

6. International transfers

Because we may work with clients and service providers in different countries, information may be processed outside your country of residence. Where applicable, we take steps required by relevant privacy laws to protect international transfers.

7. Retention and security

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including project delivery, record-keeping, legal compliance and dispute resolution. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no website, transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your privacy choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of or restriction of certain personal information, object to certain processing, request portability, or withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding.

9. Third-party websites and platforms

Our website may link to third-party platforms such as messaging, social media, ecommerce platforms or payment services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies. Please review those policies before submitting information to them.

10. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where parental consent is required. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us so we can review it.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as our services, technology or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top indicates when the current version was published.

12. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact De Impresa at support@de-impresa.com or WhatsApp/phone at +234 707 929 7488.
