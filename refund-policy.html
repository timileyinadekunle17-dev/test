Refund Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This Refund Policy explains how De Impresa handles cancellations, payments and refund requests for our ecommerce design, development, optimisation, SEO, email marketing and related digital services.

1. Project-specific payment terms

The payment schedule, deposit, milestones, deliverables and any special cancellation or refund conditions for your project are set out in the accepted proposal, invoice or written agreement. Those project-specific terms take priority where they address a matter differently from this general Policy.

2. Before work begins

If you request cancellation before work has started, contact us as soon as possible. We will review the payment received, any payment-provider charges, and any non-recoverable costs already approved or incurred. Any amount refundable will be confirmed in writing.

3. After work has started

Once work has begun, a refund is not automatic. We may account for work completed, time committed, milestones delivered, resources allocated and approved third-party costs. Where a project is cancelled, we will explain any amount due or any amount that may be refundable, in accordance with the project agreement and applicable law.

4. Deposits and milestone payments

Deposits and milestone payments reserve project capacity or cover work at the relevant stage. Whether any part is refundable depends on the agreed terms, whether work has started, costs incurred and applicable law. A deposit will not be described as non-refundable unless that condition was clearly agreed before payment and is lawful.

5. Revisions and service concerns

If you believe a deliverable does not match the agreed scope, notify us in writing with specific details and supporting examples. We will review the concern and, where appropriate, work with you to correct an issue that falls within the agreed scope. Requests for new features, changed direction or work beyond the agreed scope may require an additional fee.

6. Non-refundable third-party charges

Payments made to third-party providers, including ecommerce platforms, hosting companies, domain registrars, app developers, advertising platforms, themes or software vendors, are governed by those providers’ own refund policies. We cannot promise a refund for charges that are paid directly to and controlled by a third party.

7. How to request a refund or cancellation

Email contact@detruthagency.com with the subject “Refund or Cancellation Request”. Include your name, project or invoice reference, payment date, amount paid and a clear explanation of your request. You may also contact us at +234 704 279 3530. Please do not send full card numbers, passwords or other sensitive payment credentials.

8. Review and processing

We will acknowledge your request and review it against the project agreement, work completed, payment records and applicable law. If a refund is approved, it will normally be returned through the original payment method where practical. Processing times may depend on the payment provider or bank. We will communicate the decision and any relevant next steps.

9. Chargebacks and payment disputes

If you have a concern about a charge, please contact us first so we can attempt to resolve it. This does not limit any rights you may have to raise a dispute with your payment provider or under applicable law.

10. Your statutory rights

This Policy does not limit consumer or other legal rights that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted. Where mandatory law gives you a right to a refund or other remedy, that law will apply.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. The version published on this website applies to new requests, subject to the terms already agreed for an existing project and any mandatory legal requirements.

12. Contact

For refund or cancellation questions, contact contact@detruthagency.com.
