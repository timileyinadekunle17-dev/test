How we charge

Commission — we grow when you profit.

With the commission model, you don't pay a big upfront service fee. We take a share of net profit, so we only earn commission when your store makes money.

1. $100 commitment fee — before work starts

Every commission project starts with a one-time commitment fee. It confirms your project and lets us reserve the team for your store.

Direct bank transfer

We send the bank details when you're ready.

2. Commission rates: 3%, 5%, 10%

The rate depends on your store's net profit and the work involved. We agree the rate with you in writing before we begin.
